# OKX Mobile Reference Study

## Sources

- Official OKX download page: the app positions advanced market data and broad
  token/trading-pair access as the core mobile value.
- Official OKX C2C guide: current mobile entry paths prioritize buy crypto,
  on-chain deposit, favorites, mainstream coins, hot lists, gainers, and new
  listings.
- Official OKX mobile derivatives guide: the order panel exposes leverage, order
  type, take-profit/stop-loss, margin, liquidation price, positions, and account
  management in one compact workflow.
- Current official/help screenshots: home commonly combines account valuation,
  primary funding actions, promotion/insight content, compact feature access, and
  ranked market lists.

## Layout Patterns To Adopt

- Put account value and daily performance before editorial content.
- Give buy/deposit actions stronger visual weight than secondary products.
- Use a compact utility row for scan, search, messages, and support.
- Organize broad product capability behind a dense but readable icon grid.
- Present market discovery as tabbed ranked lists instead of decorative cards.
- Keep trading controls, risk information, and positions in one vertical work
  surface.
- Use persistent bottom navigation with five stable product areas.

## Visual Patterns To Adopt

- Black, white, and neutral gray form the main hierarchy.
- Use near-square modules, 4-8 px radii, thin dividers, and restrained shadows.
- Use large bold numbers for balances and prices; supporting copy stays small.
- Reserve green/red for market direction and buy/sell semantics.
- Motion should clarify transitions or live data, not dominate the home screen.

## Boundaries

- Do not copy OKX branding, logo, proprietary illustrations, copy, or exact
  screen composition.
- Keep HIPPO naming, Lucide icon system, backend product map, and local simulated
  data.
- Preserve the existing five-tab navigation contract.
